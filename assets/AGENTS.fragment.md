## Website growth workflow

For website strategy, SEO, conversion, performance, audit, recovery, or growth-experiment tasks, read the canonical website-growth-engine/SKILL.md first. Then read .growth/state.json, .growth/brief.json, and the current evidence before repeating work. Load only the specialist module routed by the current mode or diagnosis.

Optimize the stated business outcome and guardrails, protect valuable existing assets, preserve the distinction between IMPLEMENTED, TESTED, OBSERVED, ESTIMATED, HYPOTHESIS, and UNKNOWN, and retain evidence. Follow repository instructions and approval boundaries. Do not deploy, alter production indexing, remove pages, spend, contact third parties, or submit real transactions without approval.
