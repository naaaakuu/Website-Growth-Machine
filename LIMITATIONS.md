# Limitations and Non-Guarantees

This operating system improves process quality and evidence discipline. It cannot:

- guarantee rankings, indexing, clicks, traffic, backlinks, AI citations, conversion lift, revenue, or retention;
- see private accounts, customer conversations, deployment state, analytics, CRM/orders, search-console data, field data, or paid keyword tools without authorized access;
- replace qualified legal, medical, financial, security, privacy, accessibility, editorial, or local-regulatory review;
- infer proprietary search or answer-engine ranking weights;
- establish causal impact without an appropriately designed experiment or scoped evidence;
- turn a Lighthouse report, static crawl, browser screenshot, or code review into a full accessibility, security, SEO, or release certification;
- replace real customer research, operating capacity, fulfillment quality, product-market fit, or trustworthy first-party data;
- safely deploy, change DNS/indexing, spend money, send outreach, submit real forms, transact, or configure ongoing monitoring without explicit authorization;
- preserve secrets or private customer data safely inside version-controlled .growth records.

Scripts are deterministic but bounded. audit_site.py performs a limited same-origin HTTP crawl and does not execute JavaScript, authenticate, emulate users, validate business logic, discover every URL, or judge legal/semantic quality. evaluate_lighthouse.py validates saved report structure against a declared policy but does not run Lighthouse, prove report provenance, or establish field performance. State tools validate record shape/evidence links, not factual truth.

Use the package as a disciplined operating system, then obtain the right tools, specialists, approvals, customer evidence, and observation window for any claim beyond its tested scope.
