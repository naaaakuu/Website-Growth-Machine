# Retention and Lifetime Value

Load when the real outcome continues after a form, booking, purchase, or initial fulfillment.

## Map the complete value path

Map visitor → lead/customer → qualification → booking/purchase → fulfillment → satisfaction → repeat/cross-sell where appropriate → referral/review. Identify owners, systems, capacity, and handoffs at every stage.

Evaluate lead response, routing, confirmation, reminders, attendance, support, delivery, cancellations, refunds, reordering, loyalty, feedback, referrals, reviews, and operational capacity. A lead is not customer value until relevant downstream evidence is observed.

## Measure honestly

Use actual cohort definitions, windows, costs, refund handling, contribution/margin where available, and identity limitations. Separate booked from collected revenue and historical LTV from modeled future value. Do not use a universal response-time or retention target; derive service expectations from the business and customer need.

## Improve without harm

Use truthful, consent-appropriate follow-up and clear cancellation/unsubscribe paths. Do not increase nominal conversion by hiding eligibility, fees, conditions, or delivery constraints. If an intervention strains service capacity, revise the offer or targeting before scaling acquisition.
