# Competitor Intelligence

Load when competitor evidence could alter positioning, scope, content, product experience, or distribution.

## Compare the task, not copied artifacts

Identify direct alternatives, result-page competitors, comparison sites, marketplaces, forums, and non-search alternatives. For each sampled competitor, record observed customer job coverage, page/tool format, proof, freshness, price/eligibility clarity, UX/task completion, accessibility/performance observations, distribution signals, and gaps that matter to a visitor.

Never copy prose, graphics, data, code, reviews, proprietary scores, or private information. Do not infer traffic, revenue, link intent, or ranking causes from public inspection alone.

## Turn observations into choices

Classify a gap as:

- table stakes that must be competently met;
- a useful differentiator that the business can truthfully sustain;
- an opportunity for an original asset;
- a non-actionable feature that would add cost or risk;
- UNKNOWN pending customer or first-party evidence.

Prioritize the smallest defensible advantage. “They have more pages” is not a strategy. Record contrary evidence and why the selected approach is better for the customer/job.
