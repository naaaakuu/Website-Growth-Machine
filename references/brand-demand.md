# Brand Demand and Repeat Use

Load when generic search alone is insufficient, or when improving branded demand, memory, reputation, direct/repeat use, reviews, referrals, or owned audiences.

## Build a reason to return or recommend

Evaluate product/service usefulness, distinctive assets, consistent identity, clear expectations, expertise, transparent proof, customer support, referrals, reviews, PR, partnerships, community participation, email/owned audience, social/video formats, and offline-to-online journeys.

Use factual brand consistency across owned properties. Track branded query trends, direct/repeat behavior, opt-in audience engagement, review/referral quality, and downstream customer value with clear measurement limits. Do not label all direct traffic as brand demand.

## Integrity rules

Never purchase/fabricate reviews, endorsements, press, citations, customer activity, or experts. Do not manipulate review gating or suppress legitimate complaints. Obtain permission for customer stories and keep incentives transparent where required.

The goal is a valuable experience people choose to seek again, not a superficial increase in branded keywords.
