# SERP Intelligence

Load before making a material organic-search investment or interpreting ranking/click changes.

## Inspect the actual result environment

For each meaningful query cluster, capture the query, market, language, device, location, date/time, and source of observation. When available, record organic results, ads, local pack, shopping, AI answers, snippets, People Also Ask, video, image, forum/community, news, comparison/directory results, dominant brands, domain diversity, formats, freshness, title/snippet patterns, and apparent intent.

Do not infer proprietary ranking weights or say a result ranks because of one observed feature. Separate:

- OBSERVATION: a result feature or page trait was present;
- CORRELATION: traits co-occurred in a sample;
- HYPOTHESIS: a change might improve relevance or click usefulness;
- EVIDENCE: a controlled or otherwise scoped result supports a claim.

## Evaluate click opportunity

Ask both “can we provide a superior result?” and “would a strong result plausibly receive valuable clicks?” Maps, ads, shopping, answers, and zero-click features can alter the opportunity. Do not call a traditional rank estimate a click forecast.

Use the SERP template. Note volatile/localized results and sampling limitations. Capture a screenshot or saved export only when permitted and useful.

## Decision

Choose a page, tool, local presence, feed, different channel, or no-build option based on customer task, click opportunity, distinct value, and downstream outcome—not query volume alone.
