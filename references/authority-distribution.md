# Authority and Distribution

Load for partnerships, outreach, digital PR, link acquisition, community participation, creator/expert work, or channel design.

## Choose a genuine distribution mechanism

For each channel specify target audience, asset, recipient value, distribution method, owner, cost, consent/legal constraints, evidence, downstream outcome, and guardrails. Favor useful original work, relevant partnerships, genuine communities, owned audiences, and transparent expert/creator relationships.

Prepare a targeted rationale or draft only within authorization. Do not send bulk outreach, buy ranking links, manufacture press, trade links for ranking manipulation, create fake communities, or misrepresent relationships. Disclose material commercial relationships.

## Link and referral quality

Evaluate a prospective mention or referral by visitor relevance, editorial independence, transparency, accessibility, and downstream quality—not raw count. A link can be useful to a reader without proving ranking impact. Do not force exact-match anchor text, reciprocal schemes, or sitewide cross-links.

## Learning loop

Track eligible reach, qualified referrals, assisted outcomes, cost, reputation effects, and maintenance. Separate observed referral traffic from incremental causal impact. Stop a channel when it cannot responsibly sustain value or violates guardrails.
