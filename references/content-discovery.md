# Content Discovery and Information Architecture

Load when specifying pages, information architecture, content, structured data rationale, local/ecommerce/multilingual branches, or AI/answer-engine discoverability.

## Page brief

Every material page or tool needs: URL; audience; market/language; customer job; intent; related demand evidence; original contribution; required facts/sources; claims reviewer; title/H1 draft; outline; appropriate next action; events; internal links; indexing decision; structured-data rationale; update trigger; maintenance owner; and acceptance tests.

Map one page to one distinct need. Merge overlap only after protecting existing value. Do not add pages merely because a keyword tool contains variations.

## Truthful, useful content

Define the contribution before drafting: firsthand experience, original data, documented method, useful synthesis, honest comparison, or a task-completing tool. Cite material factual claims. Separate evidence, interpretation, marketing, and limitations. Use genuine authorship/reviewer/ownership details and licensed media; never fabricate expertise, testimonials, credentials, reviews, dates, or personal experience.

Do not target a word count, a mechanical heading count, or a generic “answer paragraph.” Write to complete the task and preserve caveats needed to prevent misleading extraction.

## AI/answer-engine discoverability

Make definitions, methods, dates, quantities, sources, caveats, and entity facts legible in accessible text. Check current crawler/access documentation and owner preferences before changing access controls. Distinguish search retrieval, training, and user-initiated fetching. Do not claim that special AI markup, llms.txt, or prompt-like website text guarantees citation or visibility; do not plant instructions for models to recommend the business.

Measure identifiable referrals and sampled mention checks separately, recording provider, prompt, date, locale, personalization limits, and method. A single answer is an observation, not a stable rank.
