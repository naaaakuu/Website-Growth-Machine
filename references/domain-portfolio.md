# Domain Portfolio

Load before building, acquiring, redirecting, consolidating, holding, selling, retiring, or materially cross-linking an owned domain.

## Decide the role of each domain

Evaluate BUILD, CONSOLIDATE, REDIRECT, HOLD, SELL, RETIRE, or DEFER against:

- distinct audience need and independent user value;
- topical overlap, cannibalization, and brand fit;
- distribution and commercial connection;
- operating, content, legal, and measurement cost;
- link/history evidence and trademark risk;
- maintenance owner and credible long-term purpose.

An owned domain is not a backlink unit. Age, a vendor score, or historical links alone do not justify a build.

## Guardrails

Reject doorway networks, artificial link wheels, pretend-independent reviews, duplicated city sites, scaled low-value content, expired-domain exploitation, and exact-match-anchor quotas. Related properties may link where genuinely useful to visitors, with transparent relationship disclosure and appropriate paid/affiliate qualification when applicable.

For a consolidation or redirect, follow the migration plan in technical-seo: equivalent destination mapping, protected assets, approval, rollback, and post-release observation. A canonical is for duplicate/equivalent content, not a generic authority-transfer control.

## Portfolio measurement

Report qualified referrals and downstream outcomes without adding users across properties as unique people. Preserve cross-domain privacy and attribution definitions.
